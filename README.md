
# HR Analytics Dashboard | Tableau

**Tableau · HR Analytics · Employee Attrition · Workforce Analysis · Data Visualization**

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze workforce demographics, employee attrition, and job satisfaction.

The dashboard helps HR teams monitor employee departures, explore workforce patterns, and identify areas requiring further retention analysis.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor employee headcount and attrition.
- Analyze departures by department and gender.
- Explore attrition patterns across age groups and education fields.
- Examine job satisfaction ratings by job role.
- Support data-driven employee retention strategies.

## Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Employees | 1,480 |
| Attrition Count | 238 |
| Attrition Rate | 16.1% |
| Active Employees | 1,242 |
| Average Employee Age | 37 |

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Employee Attrition

The dashboard reports 238 employee departures out of 1,480 employees, representing an attrition rate of 16.1%.

### 2. Attrition by Department

| Department | Employee Departures |
|---|---:|
| R&D | 133 |
| Sales | 93 |
| Human Resources | 12 |

R&D accounts for 55.9% of recorded departures.

This represents the largest share of departures, not necessarily the highest department-specific attrition rate.

### 3. Attrition by Gender

- Male employees: 151 departures
- Female employees: 87 departures

Comparing attrition rates requires considering the total employee population of each gender.

### 4. Attrition by Education Field

The highest departure counts were recorded in:

- Life Sciences: 89
- Medical: 63
- Marketing: 36
- Technical Degree: 32

### 5. Job Satisfaction Analysis

The dashboard includes a job satisfaction matrix across job roles and satisfaction levels, enabling HR teams to examine the distribution of employee satisfaction.
